\begin{abstract}
Standard physics treats time as a flat, one-dimensional coordinate parameter ticked off by external clocks across a static four-dimensional block manifold \cite{einstein1905,einstein1916}. Building upon the coherence-bounded operational spacetime framework \cite{damasio_p15,damasio_p16,damasio_p17}, this paper introduces an ontological and geometric distinction between \emph{temporal topography}---the local relief and gradients of intensive temporal cadence density ($\rho_\tau$)---and \emph{temporal topology}---the connectivity and pinch-offs of phase-locked Operational Simultaneity Horizons ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}$). Using solar-system isoline and relief mappings, we show that mass-energy ($E = mc^2$) ensures $\rho_\tau > 0$ everywhere above a Planck coherence floor ($\Phi_{\mathrm{floor}}$), driving gravitation as an internal topographic cadence gradient ($\nabla \rho_\tau$) and confining active informational complexity to a bounded intermediate elevation band. We further demonstrate how the Higgs condensate ($v \approx 246\,\mathrm{GeV}$) \cite{higgs1964,englert1964} anchors clockless conformal modes into massive rest-frame horizons ($m_0 = h f_{\mathrm{local}}/c^2$), while massless photons ($d\tau = 0$, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} \to 0$) traverse low-cadence valleys via conserved Coherent Temporal Action ($h \cdot \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{eff}}$). Finally, contrasting a null photon transit against an orbiting observer's metric clock exposes the epistemological trap of mathematical equivalence ($=$), separating detached formal reversibility from one-way physical actualization \cite{frege1918}.
\end{abstract}

\section{From Clocks to Cadence: The Four-Stage Conceptual Bridge}

Classical kinematics and relativistic block-universe interpretations share an unexamined premise: that time is an extensive, uniform coordinate line ($t$) measured by external clocks embedded in a pre-existing four-dimensional spatial container \cite{einstein1905}. \begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{The Phase-Locked Horizon Stage:} Extensive duration and spatial distance are recognized as emergent properties confined to local Operational Simultaneity Horizons ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}(r) > 0$), where coherent rhythms lock into a shared plane of interaction between an observer (\emph{Subject as Subject}, $t_{\mathrm{obs}}$) and an inspected system (\emph{Object as Subject}, $t_{\mathrm{pre}}$) \cite{damasio_p15}.
    \item \textbf{The Horizon-Shedding Limit ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} \to 0$):} Stripping away metric rulers and rest-frame clocks ($d\tau = 0$) dissolves extensive geometry without annihilating time, leaving pure, un-anchored temporal rhythm intact \cite{damasio_p17}.
    \item \textbf{Clockless Intensive Cadence ($\rho_\tau$):} Time is revealed fundamentally as intensive temporal cadence---a qualitative pulse density that requires neither clock nor spatial ruler, actualizing strictly within thick, complex present moments ($dt \not\to 0$) drawn one-way from an immediate pool of possibilities.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Epistemological Trap of Mathematical Equivalence}

The illusion of physical time-reversibility ($t \to -t$) never arises inside a photon's clockless regime ($d\tau = 0$), where no extensive duration exists to invert. Rather, it emerges when an observer anchored inside a metric horizon ($t_{\mathrm{obs}}$) conflates detached formal \emph{rationality} with infallible ontic \emph{reason} \cite{frege1918}.

Consider an astronaut aboard the International Space Station observing a solar photon cross the interplanetary valley to Earth. While the photon transfers conserved action $h \cdot \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in a single clockless topological bridge, the astronaut's Higgs-anchored horizon ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} > 0$) logs roughly $499\,\mathrm{s}$ of extensive duration. Standard wave equations insert an algebraic equivalence ($=$) between emission and absorption across those $499\,\mathrm{s}$. Because symbol equivalence is symmetric, formal rationality permits inverting $t_{\mathrm{obs}} \to -t_{\mathrm{obs}}$, falsely implying that an absorbed photon can un-actualize from Earth and return to the Sun. In reality, mathematical equivalence tracks conserved action across an irreversible, one-way actualization from the immediate pool of possibilities.
