\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

This treatise presents the formal axiomatic architecture of the
\textbf{AEON-\(T_{\mu\nu}\) \& Philosophy of Time} framework as a
self-contained deductive system. Moving systematically from
pre-axiomatic ontology to operational physical mathematics, the work is
organized into three interlocking tiers: (I) \textbf{Five Foundational
Postulates} defining the relationship between pre-quantitative Time and
Space, ontological determinations (\(\mathcal{D}_\infty\)
vs.~\(\varnothing\)), intrinsic clock complexity, causal finitude, and
open-system condensation; (II) an \textbf{Individual Architectural
Lexicon of Eleven Operational Metrics} (\(A_s^v\), \(\rho_\tau\),
\(dt_{\mathrm{pre}}\), \(\Delta\tau_{\mathrm{sig}}\),
\(\Gamma_{\mathrm{tot}}\), \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}\),
\(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{loc}}\), \(\Omega\), \(\Phi_\tau\),
\(\mathcal{W}(C)\), \(\Xi_{\mathrm{QG}}\), and \(\zeta\)), specifying
the exact mathematical domain, boundary limits, and philosophical
warrant of each operator; and (III) \textbf{Five Fundamental Laws}
governing universal clockhood, bounded operational simultaneity,
diachronic thermodynamic irreversibility, two-stage path-adequate
condensation, and semiclassical horizon closure. By anchoring every
quantitative operator to explicit ontological and epistemic boundary
conditions, the framework preserves the empirical machinery of general
relativity, gauge theory, and open quantum mechanics while eliminating
unphysical continuum divergences, instantaneous global foliations, and
timeless reversible loops.
