* **Human Cortical Integration Window ($\tau_{\mathrm{bind}} \approx 100\,\mathrm{ms}$):** Because the observer's thalamocortical simultaneity horizon ($L_{\mathrm{sim}}^{\mathrm{cortex}} > L_{\mathrm{cortex}}$) integrates sensory arrivals across roughly $10^7$ times the $10\,\mathrm{ns}$ optical transit time, the discrete causal buildup of illumination is smoothed into a single seamless perceptual "Now."

## 3. Reflection, Color, Polarization & Shadow Occlusion

Every ordinary object inside the $3\,\mathrm{m}$ room acts as a specialized boundary operator acting on the incoming wave manifold:
