\begin{abstract}
This treatise establishes the deductive philosophical architecture linking pre-quantitative infinities, formal logic, and theoretical physics within the $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$ framework. Beginning from the undivided, essentially homogeneous infinities of Time and Space that ground all subjective experience and the universal subject--object relation in science, we trace how the ontological requirement to express reality simultaneously as quantifiable finite determinations and within an infinite spatio-temporal horizon generates formal logic itself. By distinguishing absolute ontological absence ($\varnothing_{\mathrm{ont}}$) from relative quantitative zero ($0$) inside the Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$), we formalize an intensional logic of \emph{path-adequacy} and \emph{diachronic identity} ($A_s^v$), proving why cyclic state alternation ($A_1^1 \to A_2^1 \to A_1^2 \to A_2^2 \dots$) entails irreversible decoherent accumulation ($\Delta\Omega > 0$) and renders perpetual motion machines ontologically impossible. Pushing this logic to its full structural expression isolates \emph{functional infinity} ($\Phi_\tau = 1$) as the indispensable abstract bridge compressing unbounded determinations into lawful possibility manifolds prior to open-system condensation into fuzzy, minimal-entropy records ($A_s^v$). Finally, we demonstrate the holistic reach of this deductive chain across two fundamental domains: (i) interpreting local gauge symmetry as surplus coordinate determinations inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$ and Aharonov--Bohm holonomy as physical path-adequacy; and (ii) delineating the cosmological threshold before, during, and after the Big Bang—contrasting the clockless, circumstantially homogeneous single state ($A = A_1^1$, possessing real temporal intensity $\rho_\tau \in \mathcal{D}_\infty$ without metric duration $t_{\mathrm{pre}}$) against post-condensation diachronic history.
\end{abstract}

\section{Where We Start: Pre-Quantitative Infinities and the Ground of Experience}

Any rigorous ontology of physical reality must begin not with axiomatic symbols, which are already late-stage abstractions, but with the irreducible conditions under which anything can be experienced, registered, or inquired into at all. Across the $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$ corpus, the deductive trajectory unfolds through five sequential projections:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Pre-Quantitative Infinities as the Foundation of Experience and Science:} The original infinities of Time and Space—completely homogeneous within and non-quantitative—form the undivided horizon of all subjective experience, including the universal subject--object relationship upon which every scientific measurement depends.
    \item \textbf{The Double Requirement of Ontological Logic:} Explaining the logic of ontology through physics requires holding two poles together simultaneously: reality must be expressed through \emph{quantifiable finite determinations} while continuing to subsist within the \emph{infinite temporal and spatial horizon of the subject}.
    \item \textbf{The Genesis of Formal Logic:} Logic is neither an arbitrary linguistic game nor an autonomous Platonic realm suspended outside reality; it arises directly from the structural interplay between the infinite homogeneous ground and finite quantized determinations, expressing both.
    \item \textbf{Full Expression Yielding Functional Infinity and Diachronic Identity:} Carrying this logic to its complete structural conclusion inevitably yields \emph{functional infinity} ($\Phi_\tau = 1$) as a lawful compression bridge and \emph{diachronic identity} ($A_s^v$) as the ordinal grammar of fuzzy actualization.
    \item \textbf{The Epistemic--Ontic Audit:} Keeping the entire sequence tied together provides an unambiguous diagnostic for distinguishing statements about \emph{reality itself} from statements about \emph{how bounded subjects partition, interact with, and express it}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Essential Homogeneity vs. Heterogeneous Mathematical Infinities}

A foundational error in modern mathematical physics lies in conflating the \emph{original infinities of Time and Space} with the \emph{transfinite and continuous infinities of set theory}. Mathematical infinities—whether Cantorian cardinals ($\aleph_0, \aleph_1, \dots$) or real-line continua—are constructed out of \emph{heterogeneous partitions}: every element or subset inside a mathematical set is either strictly distinct from another ($x \neq y$) or collapses into set-theoretic identity ($x = y$). Mathematics cannot operate without carving internal boundaries.

By contrast, pre-metric Time and Space are \textbf{essentially homogeneous within}. They possess no internal seams, no zero-width mathematical points $(x_0, t_0)$, and no pre-installed metric rulers. Whenever differential geometry models spacetime as an uncountable manifold of zero-dimensional point-events $(\mathcal{M}, g_{\mu\nu})$, it overlays a heterogeneous set-theoretic scaffold onto an undivided, non-quantitative ground. Consequently, it remains permanently impossible for mathematics to capture the original infinities as quantities; mathematical infinities measure our partitions, never the homogeneous horizon that makes partitioning possible.

\subsection{Absence of Determinations ($\varnothing_{\mathrm{ont}}$) vs. Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$)}

Prior to the introduction of mathematical axioms, metaphysics delivers two complementary ontological infinities to the doorstep of foundational logic:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Absence of Determinations ($\varnothing_{\mathrm{ont}}$):} Pure ontological nothingness—the total absence of relation, boundary, referential frame, or registration.
    \item \textbf{The Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$):} The inexhaustible totality of everything that denotes \emph{something} in opposition to no determination whatsoever. Inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$, every distinct specification of reality—physical interactions, perceptual judgments (\emph{``I think there are five apples in the basket''}), retrospective beliefs (\emph{``I believe there were five apples in the basket''}), multi-agent testimonies, and distinct arithmetical paths ($2 - 1$ versus $3 - 2$)—occupies its own irreducible place.
\end{itemize}

This distinction exposes a persistent equivocation in classical arithmetic: the use of a single symbol (`$0$') to denote both \textbf{relative privation inside an actualized determination} (\emph{nothing}) and \textbf{absolute ontological absence} (\emph{nothingness}). Suppose Adam brings a basket containing five apples ($A_5^1$) and Billy removes all five apples, leaving the empty basket ($A_0^2$). Writing `$0$ apples' presupposes an extensive scaffold of active determinations—Adam, Billy, the physical basket, spatial containment, and the category \emph{apple}. Here, `$0$' is a positive quantitative determination ($s = 0$) sitting legitimately on the integer line ($-1, 0, +1$) inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$. Conversely, if neither Adam, nor the basket, nor any spatial frame exists, we confront $\varnothing_{\mathrm{ont}}$—which is not a number, cannot be enclosed in set-theoretic brackets (`$\{\varnothing\}$') to manufacture ordinal `$1$', and cannot precede physical existence in a causal sequence.

\section{How Logic Arises: Intensional Path-Adequacy and Diachronic Identity ($A_s^v$)}

Formal logic emerges at the exact threshold where bounded subjects must express finite, quantifiable determinations while anchored within the infinite horizon of Time and Space. When foundational mathematics steps up to the doorstep of $\mathcal{D}_\infty$, its axioms function as a \textbf{lossy compression sieve}: by enforcing extensional equality ($a = b$), mathematics discards an infinitely large class of qualitative, doxastic, and historical determinations in order to achieve context-free calculability.

\subsection{Path-Adequacy ($5 - 5 = 0$ vs. $6 - 6 = 0$) and the Axiomatic Sieve}

In classical extensional arithmetic, $5 - 5 = 0$ and $6 - 6 = 0$ (or $2 - 1 = 1$ and $3 - 2 = 1$) collapse into identical numerical residues, erasing the historical path by which the state was determined. Inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$, however, **path-adequacy** is essential: while $5 - 5 = 0$ adequately represents the diachronic trajectory of Adam's basket ($A_5^1 \to A_0^2$), $6 - 6 = 0$ is ontologically inadequate because it falsifies the initial actualized state. By remembering what the axiomatic sieve throws away, pre-axiomatic logic prevents stripped-down numerical residues from masquerading as complete ontological histories.

\subsection{Modal and Doxastic Structure of Diachronic Identity ($A_s^v$)}

Classical predicate logic treats identity ($A = A$) as an anachronic, cost-free equivalence outside time, creating the familiar Parmenidean--Heraclitean deadlock: under Leibniz's Law, any change destroys identity, while strict identity forbids change. In $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$, every actualized entity is indexed by both a **qualitative determination subscript ($s$)** and an **ordinal temporal re-actualization superscript ($v$)**, written $A_s^v$:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Minimal-Entropy Coarse-Graining ($A_1^1$):} Any actual physical object possesses inexhaustible microscopic and relational multiplicity. Registering an entity as $A_1^1$ represents a subjective and operational determination—the \emph{minimal-entropy coarse-graining} sufficient to stabilize $A$ as a determinate object without exhausting its underlying complexity.
    \item \textbf{Permanence as Ordinal Advance ($A_1^1 \to A_1^2 \to A_1^3$):} Even when an entity's qualitative determination remains invariant ($s = 1$), persistence across time requires continuous step-by-step ordinal re-actualization ($v \to v+1$) paced by finite causal signaling ($\Delta\tau_{\mathrm{sig}}$) and proper lapse ($dt_{\mathrm{pre}}$). \emph{Permanence equals change.}
    \item \textbf{Epistemic and Doxastic Shift ($A_1^1 \to A_2^1$):} Transitioning from $s = 1$ to $s = 2$ encodes an explicit comparative modal judgment: \emph{``We checked at an earlier ordinal step and determined $A$ differently; we now register or represent $A$ under a distinct determination.''}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cyclic State Alternation and the Ontological Impossibility of Perpetual Motion}

Consider a physical oscillator or cyclic engine that alternates strictly between two qualitative states:
\begin{equation}
    A_1^1 \longrightarrow A_2^1 \longrightarrow A_1^2 \longrightarrow A_2^2 \longrightarrow A_1^3 \longrightarrow A_2^3 \longrightarrow \cdots
\end{equation}
Although the qualitative determination repeats ($s = 1 \to 2 \to 1$), the ordinal temporal index $v$ advances irreversibly at every transition; there is no physical path returning to $A_1^1$. Because each transition requires causal interaction across proper lapse $dt_{\mathrm{pre}}$ within an open environment characterized by total dissipation rate $\Gamma_{\mathrm{tot}} = \Gamma_{\mathrm{th}} + \Gamma_{\mathrm{grav}}$, the system monotonically accumulates decoherent coupling action:
\begin{equation}
    \Delta\Omega = \int \Gamma_{\mathrm{tot}}\,dt_{\mathrm{pre}} > 0.
\end{equation}
Consequently, the system's coherence functional $\Phi_\tau(\Omega)$ can only decay toward its thermal floor ($\Phi_\tau \to \Phi_{\mathrm{floor}}$) unless continuously driven by an external free-energy gradient. A perpetual motion machine would require $A_1^1 \to A_2^1 \to A_1^1$—erasing ordinal succession ($v$) and reversing $\Delta\Omega$. Thus, the Second Law of Thermodynamics is not merely statistical improbability; it is the physical expression of irreversible ordinal becoming.

\section{Functional Infinity: The Bridge Between Metaphysics and Physics}

A concept positioned directly at the interface between ontology and theoretical physics faces a dual hazard: to a physicist, \emph{functional infinity} may appear as idle philosophical decoration beyond standard Hilbert space; to a metaphysicist, it may look as though it already resides inside empirical physics. In truth, **functional infinity ($\Phi_\tau = 1$)** is an indispensable theoretical and abstract bridge that neither discipline can dispense with if it seeks explanatory closure.

\subsection{The Two-Stage Compression Funnel}

Functional infinity operates as the exact intermediate hinge connecting pre-quantitative ontological infinities to finite, condensed physical records through a two-stage compression funnel:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Stage \arabic*:}]
    \item \textbf{Lawful Constriction ($\mathcal{D}_\infty \to \Phi_\tau = 1$, Maximal Multiplicity):} Starting from an actualized state $A_1^1$, the laws of physics and local boundary conditions act as a structural filter on the unbounded Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$). They constrict all conceivable trajectories down to a \emph{bounded manifold of physically admissible successor states}—the uncoupled wave manifold inhabiting functional infinity ($\Phi_\tau = 1$). Within this manifold, all lawful possibilities remain open simultaneously, representing \emph{maximal informational multiplicity} prior to environmental condensation.
    \item \textbf{Open-System Condensation ($\Phi_\tau \to \Phi_{\mathrm{floor}} \to A_s^v$, Minimal Entropy):} Through finite causal interaction and open-system bath coupling ($\Omega \ge 1$), the maximal-multiplicity wave manifold condenses irreversibly into \emph{one actualized successor state} ($A_1^2$ or $A_2^1$). Because physical systems retain irreducible microscopic complexity across nested scales, this condensed record is never a zero-width mathematical point; it is inherently \emph{fuzzy}—a coarse-grained set of \emph{minimal entropy} sufficient to anchor a determinate observable ($s$).
\end{enumerate}

Without functional infinity as an ontologically real, pre-condensed manifold of lawful possibilities, physics is forced into false dilemmas—either treating the wave function as mere subjective ignorance (which fails to explain interference and topological phase shifts) or inflating every uncondensed possibility into an unobservable parallel universe. Conversely, without functional infinity acting as a lawful filter, metaphysics cannot account for why discrete actualizations ($A_s^v$) obey strict conservation laws and symmetries rather than manifesting arbitrary chaos.

\section{Natural Application I: Gauge Theory and Topological Holonomy}

Because this philosophical architecture audits how finite determinations are carved out of homogeneous Space-Time, it immediately illuminates **gauge theory** ($U(1)$ electromagnetism, non-Abelian Yang--Mills fields, and diffeomorphism invariance) without reifying mathematical redundancies into physical substances.

\subsection{Local Gauge Symmetry as Surplus Determinations in $\mathcal{D}_\infty$}

In classical electrodynamics and quantum field theory, the four-potential $A_\mu$ exhibits local gauge redundancy: transforming $A_\mu \to A_\mu + \partial_\mu \lambda(x)$ leaves the local field-strength tensor $F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu$ invariant. Why must theoretical physics rely on redundant potentials $A_\mu$ if only gauge-invariant quantities are observable?

Through the lens of $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$, **gauge transformations are distinct symbolic determinations inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$**—strictly analogous to $2 - 1$ and $3 - 2$—that project onto the exact same condensed observable ($A_s^v$). Because pre-metric Space and Time are essentially homogeneous, nature provides no built-in global coordinate frame across separated regions. To write local differential laws on a heterogeneous coordinate grid, human mathematics must introduce local internal reference frames (fibers) at each point, generating an infinite surplus of descriptive determinations in $\mathcal{D}_\infty$. Local gauge symmetry acts as the Stage 1 constriction filter, ensuring that the lawful possibility manifold ($\Phi_\tau = 1$) and its condensed physical records ($A_s^v$) remain invariant under arbitrary human choices of local framing.

\subsection{The Aharonov--Bohm Effect as Physical Path-Adequacy}

In the **Aharonov--Bohm effect**, an electron beam splits around a shielded magnetic solenoid where the local magnetic field vanishes identically ($B = 0$) along every point of both paths, yet the interference pattern at the detector shifts in proportion to the enclosed magnetic flux.

If physical reality were exhausted by pointwise local residues (`$B = 0$' at each coordinate point), no physical difference could exist between the two paths—just as extensional arithmetic sees no difference between $5 - 5 = 0$ and $6 - 6 = 0$. In $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$, however, the uncondensed electron wave manifold inhabits **functional infinity ($\Phi_\tau = 1$)** prior to detector condensation ($\Omega \ge 1$). Inside $\Phi_\tau = 1$, the wave registers the **global Wilson loop holonomy**:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{W}(C) = \mathrm{Tr}\,\mathcal{P}\exp\!\left(i \oint_C A_\mu\,dx^\mu\right),
\end{equation}
which integrates the non-local topological phase accumulated across the entire closed path $C$. Gauge holonomy is thus the exact physical realization of **path-adequacy**: two trajectories sharing identical pointwise residues (`$0$' local field) carry distinct historical determinations that manifest directly upon open-system condensation at the screen ($A_s^v$).

\section{Natural Application II: Before, During, and After the Big Bang}

Applying this same holistic framework to the **cosmological threshold** resolves the longstanding conceptual paradoxes surrounding the origin of time, cosmic inflation, and conformal cyclic crossovers without extrapolating equations past the Quantum-Gravity Wall ($\Xi_{\mathrm{QG}} < 1$).

\subsection{Before the Big Bang: Circumstantial Homogeneity and the Anachronic Single State ($A = A_1^1$)}

It is crucial to distinguish the **essential homogeneity** of the original pre-quantitative infinities (Time and Space) from the **circumstantial homogeneity** of a pre-Big-Bang or scale-free conformal ground state. A massless, clockless cosmic substrate is not essentially homogeneous by ontological definition; rather, it is \emph{circumstantially undifferentiated} because bound masses, spatial rulers, and periodic oscillators have dissolved—rendering any attribution of metric differentiation physically meaningless under those circumstances.

Three rigorous consequences follow:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Real Temporal Intensity Without Nothingness ($\rho_\tau \in \mathcal{D}_\infty$):} Although bound clocks do not exist, the substrate exists and possesses real \emph{objective temporal intensity} ($\rho_\tau$). It therefore belongs squarely inside the Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$), never to ontological absence ($\varnothing_{\mathrm{ont}}$).
    \item \textbf{Strictly One Anachronic State ($A = A_1^1$):} Because no physical referential exists to index ordinal transitions ($v = 1 \to 2 \to 3$), it is pointless—literally in mathematical terms—to describe pre-Big-Bang existence as diachronic. It constitutes a \textbf{single state ($A = A_1^1$)}, mirroring classical anachronic identity ($A = A$) precisely because there are no clocks. Within this regime, quantitative duration vanishes: the state can be described equally well as an infinitely small instant or an infinitely large eternity.
    \item \textbf{Internal Change as Pure Theoretical Projection:} If we hypothesize internal evolution occurring \emph{within} that circumstantially homogeneous state $A_1^1$, such change cannot be physically registered or known from inside the regime; it remains strictly an \emph{external theoretical projection} cast backward once bound referentials have emerged.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{During the Crossover: Symmetry Breaking and First Condensation ($\Phi_\tau \to \Phi_{\mathrm{floor}}$)}

As cosmological expansion and symmetry breaking cross the semiclassical boundary ($\Xi_{\mathrm{QG}} < 1$), conformal invariance breaks and massive degrees of freedom condense out of functional infinity ($\Phi_\tau = 1 \to \Phi_{\mathrm{floor}}$). The emergence of bound particles, coupled radiation baths ($\Omega \ge 1$), and finite causal horizons ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}$) establishes the first localized, fuzzy referentials—initiating the primordial transition from the anachronic single state $A_1^1$ to the first differentiated successor $A_2^2$.

\subsection{After the Threshold: Diachronic History and Metric Duration ($t_{\mathrm{pre}}$)}

Once bound clocks, spatial separations, and open-system dissipation stabilize, reality enters full **diachronic actualization** ($A_1^1 \to A_1^2 \to A_1^3 \dots$). Metric duration \emph{stricto sensu} ($t_{\mathrm{pre}} = \int \sqrt{N^2(\theta)}\,dt$) and finite round-trip signaling lags ($\Delta\tau_{\mathrm{sig}}$) become operationally well-defined, while backward coordinate extrapolations toward a zero-width singularity ($t = 0$) are exposed as heterogeneous mathematical projections pushed beyond the breakdown boundary of physical referentials.

By tracing theoretical physics back to its pre-axiomatic roots in homogeneous Time and Space and the Infinity of Determinations ($\mathcal{D}_\infty$), $\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}$ unifies ontology, modal logic, thermodynamics, gauge holonomy, and cosmology within a single disciplined grammar.
