Combining Roger Penrose's conformal spatial rescaling with two-stage operational condensation ($\Phi_\tau(\Omega)$), we trace how the massless radiation pool of a prior aeon crosses $A_2^1$ and nucleates mass-bearing bound clocks ($A_3^1$), mapping six evolutionary stages that sharply distinguish retroactive metric projections ($t_{\mathrm{retro}}$) from genuinely earned clock duration ($t_{\mathrm{earned}}$) ignited at electroweak symmetry breaking ($A = A_1^1 \to A_1^2$).

## 1. Introduction: The Chronometric Impasse at $t = 0$

Modern physical cosmology achieves extraordinary empirical precision from Big Bang Nucleosynthesis ($t \sim 1\,\mathrm{s}$) through Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) recombination ($t \sim 380{,}000\,\mathrm{yr}$) to late-time structure formation. Yet when classical General Relativity is extrapolated backward toward $t \to 0$, the formalism suffers a dual breakdown:

1. **Geometric Divergence:** Curvature invariants diverge ($R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} \to \infty$), signaling the breakdown of smooth manifold calculus ($\mathbb{R}^4$).
2. **Chronometric Circularity:** Standard accounts routinely quote timestamps such as $10^{-43}\,\mathrm{s}$ (Planck epoch) or $10^{-36}\,\mathrm{s}$ (GUT scale) as though an operational stopwatch were ticking at $t = 0$. However, physical seconds are defined by periodic transitions in bound, mass-bearing quantum systems (such as hyperfine atomic oscillators). Prior to the nucleation of rest mass and bound structures, no physical reference clocks exist to slice duration into seconds.

Traditional metaphysics compounds this difficulty through a false dichotomy: either time itself "began" out of a timeless nothingness ($\varnothing$)—leaving any causal transition into existence unintelligible—or an infinite, pre-existing clock-grid ($t \in (-\infty, +\infty)$) is projected backward into an eternal past, generating severe thermodynamic fine-tuning and Boltzmann-brain paradoxes.

Within the *Theory of Objective Time* ($\text{AEON-T}_{\mu\nu}$) [1, 2], this dilemma dissolves once we recognize that nothing exists outside of time and that our mathematical coordinate grids are secondary cartographic projections built atop an active temporal substrate. To exist at all is to possess **temporal cadence**—the intrinsic, self-expressing rhythm of objective time ($ho_	au > 0$). Metric duration ($dt_{\mathrm{pre}}$), by contrast, is an operational quantity *earned* only when bounded, open-system physical clocks (*"If it is, it is a clock"*) condense and exchange causal signals across finite separations.

## 2. Ontological Setup: The Anachronic Crossover ($A = A_1^1 \to A_2^1 \to A_3^1$)

To formalize the transition across a cosmological aeon boundary without invoking creation *ex nihilo* or unconstructible completed infinities [3], we introduce the three-stage diachronic sequence:

$$A = A_1^1 \longrightarrow A_2^1 \longrightarrow A_3^1$$

* **Subscript $s \in \{1, 2, 3\}$** indexes the **qualitative structural regime** of the cosmic cycle.
* **Superscript $v = 1$** indexes the **ordinal tick-count** within each transitional boundary state.

### 2.1 State $A_1^1$: Terminal Tail of the Outgoing Aeon
In the asymptotic far future of an expanding aeon, gravitational collapse and subsequent Hawking evaporation eliminate all black holes, while decay processes and cosmic dilution disperse remaining fermionic matter into an ultra-cold, dilute bath of massless radiation (photons and gravitons) [4]. As rest mass ($m > 0$) vanishes from the cosmic inventory, bound physical clocks and rigid measuring rods dissolve. $A_1^1$ represents the final operational boundary where metric distinctions fade out.

### 2.2 State $A_2^1$: The Pre-Metric Crossover Seam (Circumstantial Homogeneity)
Between the dissolution of outgoing clocks ($A_1^1$) and the nucleation of incoming clocks ($A_3^1$) lies $A_2^1$—a regime of **circumstantial homogeneity**. Three ontological determinations characterize $A_2^1$:

1. **Zero Bound Clocks (`0`), Never Ontological Nothingness (`∅`):** Within the domain of massive physical clocks, the count in $A_2^1$ is strictly `0` (determinate absence within a domain), never `∅` (ontological nothingness) [3]. The universe ($A = A$) continues to exist, carrying the conformally invariant radiation legacy of $A_1^1$ immersed in active temporal intensity ($ho_	au > 0$) and uncondensed potential (**Functional Infinity**, $\Omega$).
2. **Pre-Metric Scale-Indifference ($10^{-24}\,\mathrm{s} \equiv 10^{12}\,\mathrm{yr}$):** Because zero bound clocks exist inside $A_2^1$ to register internal sub-ticks ($v$ remains locked at $1$), no proper duration ($d\tau$ or $dt_{\mathrm{pre}}$) can be assigned to this state. Rescaling a numeric time label by forty orders of magnitude alters nothing physically: one yoctosecond and trillions of years are metrically identical. Simultaneously, as demonstrated by Roger Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC) [4, 5], the absence of rest mass renders Einstein's field equations conformally invariant ($g_{\mu\nu} \mapsto \Omega_{\mathrm{conf}}^2 g_{\mu\nu}$), making an infinitely diluted, cold radiation bath geometrically indistinguishable from an ultra-dense, scale-free boundary seam.
3. **Epistemological Bookkeeping:** In human temporal representation, $A_2^1$ is recorded as a single unitary ordinal step between $A_1^1$ and $A_3^1$ (formally notated as lasting "one second" or "one yoctosecond" purely as an ordinal placeholder) so that causal directionality ($A_1^1$ *before* $A_2^1$, and $A_2^1$ *before* $A_3^1$) is preserved without fabricating an infinite pre-clock timeline.

### 2.3 Why the Universe "Pops" into Re-Expansion ($A_2^1 \to A_3^1$)
In pure conformal geometry, a persistent conceptual puzzle is what drives a massless, proper-time-free boundary ($ds^2 = 0$) to transition into a new expanding aeon. Within $\text{AEON-T}_{\mu\nu}$, the mechanism is grounded in **self-expressing temporal cadence**:

* **Active Cadence ($ho_	au > 0$):** Because time is transcendental and self-referential, $ho_	au > 0$ is never a static Parmenidean block; it is active temporal actualization. Just as a photon inside the photosphere of TON 618 and a photon in empty intergalactic space share identical null proper-time intervals ($ds^2 = 0$) yet unfold under radically different temporal cadences due to ambient substrate intensity ($ho_	au$), the radiation pool in $A_2^1$ carries active, cadence-driven dynamic tension.
* **Law-Governed Filtering of Functional Infinity ($\Omega$):** Conservation laws and local interaction constraints filter the open-ended potential ($\Omega$) of $A_2^1$ into a finite set of physically viable successor configurations, driving conformal symmetry breaking via scalar/Higgs vacuum condensation ($\Phi_	au(\Omega)$).
* **Ignition of Metric Scale ($A_3^1$):** The instant rest mass ($m > 0$) condenses out of $\Omega$, bound physical clocks nucleate and physical rulers ($\Delta\ell$) switch back on inside an already-energetic, conformally compressed radiation bath.
