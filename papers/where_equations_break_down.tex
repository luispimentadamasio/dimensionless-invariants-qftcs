system and binary pulsar regimes, the Einstein field equations

infinitely long rotating cylinder, Morris--Thorne traversable wormholes
threaded by negative energy, and the deep interior region (\(r < r_-\))
inside the inner Cauchy horizon of an idealized Kerr rotating black
hole.

In standard geometric interpretation, CTCs threaten the logical
consistency of physics by permitting causal loops (``grandfather
paradoxes'') and forcing physicists to invoke ad-hoc patches such as
Hawking's \emph{Chronology Protection Conjecture} or Novikov's
\emph{Self-Consistency Principle}.
