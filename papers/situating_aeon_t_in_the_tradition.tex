\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

Across twenty-five centuries of metaphysics and modern physics,
inquiries into the nature of time have repeatedly fractured into two
opposing errors: \emph{substantivalist reification}, which mistakes
mathematical coordinate grids (\(\mathbb{R}^4\), \(g_{\mu\nu}\), \(t\))
for self-subsistent containers, and \emph{eliminativist timelessness},
which infers from the coordinate invariance of quantum-gravity
formalisms (\(H\Psi = 0\)) that temporal becoming is a subjective
illusion. Working within the Theory of Objective Time
(\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\)), this paper establishes the precise
philosophical genealogy and structural positioning of the framework
across classical metaphysics, twentieth-century process and operational
philosophy, and contemporary quantum-gravity research.

First, we formalize the \emph{focus versus default} method of mutually
co-defining first principles and distinguish inviolable \emph{Ontic
Reason} (\(X \to Y\)) from fallible human rationality, grounding
\emph{Functional Infinity} (\(\Omega\)) as the direct modal expression
of Ontic Reason anchored to persistent \emph{Diachronic Identity}
(\(A_s^v\)). Second, we resolve the historic 1922 Paris debate between
Henri Bergson and Albert Einstein through the \textbf{Three-Tier
Architecture of Time}---separating pure un-clocked temporal cadence
(\(\rho_\tau > 0\)), \emph{object-subjective time} belonging to every
bounded \emph{Center of Resolution} (\emph{object-as-subject} within a
finite Operational Simultaneity Horizon \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}\)),
and reflective conscious interiority (\emph{subject-as-subject}). Third,
we contrast \(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\) with Alfred North Whitehead's
process philosophy, Hans Reichenbach's causal signal theory, Lee
Smolin's temporal naturalism, Rafael Sorkin's causal set theory, Carlo
Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics, Wojciech Zurek's quantum
Darwinism, and String Theory. Finally, we articulate the framework's
guiding epistemological ethic---\emph{if metaphysics and empirical
physics differ, physics wins}---demonstrating how
\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\) operates as a post-relativistic operational
process ontology grounded in falsifiable laboratory boundaries.

\textbf{Keywords:} Philosophy of Time, Objective Time,

\subsection{1. Introduction: The Great Fork in the Philosophy of
Time}\label{introduction-the-great-fork-in-the-philosophy-of-time}

Every major paradigm in the history of natural philosophy has stumbled
over a single foundational question: \emph{What is the ontological
status of time and space relative to physical existence?} Across
twenty-five centuries, answers have repeatedly collapsed into one of two
symmetrical errors:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Substantivalist and Platonist Reification (Newton, Einstein's
  Block Universe, String Theory):} Coordinate manifolds
  (\(\mathbb{R}^4\)), metric tensors (\(g_{\mu\nu}\)), and linear time
  parameters (\(t\)) are treated as fundamental, self-subsistent
  containers or geometric arenas inside which matter moves. When
  extrapolated beyond the operational stability of physical clocks, this
  reification forces equations into unphysical infinities---black hole
  singularities (\(r = 0\)), initial Big Bang singularities (\(t = 0\)),
  quantum field theory ultraviolet poles (\(\Delta\ell \to 0\)), and the
  \(10^{120}\) vacuum energy catastrophe.
\item
  \textbf{Eliminativist and Illusionist Timelessness (Parmenides,
  McTaggart, Barbour, Rovelli):} Observing that external coordinate time
  \(t\) drops out of canonical quantum gravity (the Wheeler--DeWitt
  equation \(H\Psi = 0\)) and that Special Relativity forbids a global
  hyperplane of simultaneity, eliminativists conclude that \emph{time
  itself does not exist at the fundamental level}. Temporal passage,
  irreversible becoming, and the thermodynamic arrow are demoted to
  coarse-grained psychological illusions.
\end{enumerate}

The Theory of Objective Time (\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\)) dissolves this
false dichotomy by returning to an elementary ontological truth:
\textbf{Being is in itself, and because it is, it lasts (\(A_s^v\));
Time and Space are not things-in-themselves.} One never encounters a
piece of ``pure time'' or ``empty space'' anywhere in experience or
experiment; one encounters only \emph{things lasting in time} and
\emph{things situated in space}. Smooth spacetime geometry
(\(g_{\mu\nu}\)) and retroactive coordinate timelines
(\(t_{\mathrm{retro}}\)) are secondary survey maps earned by bounded,
open-system physical clocks---never the ontic substrate itself.
Conversely, when coordinate rulers dissolve at extreme curvature
(\(\Xi_{\mathrm{QG}} \ge 1\)) or across the pre-mass cosmological
crossover (\(A_2^1\)), it is merely the human cartographic grid that
halts, while objective temporal cadence (\(\rho_\tau > 0\)) persists
unbroken.

To prevent misreadings---such as mistaking \emph{Functional Infinity}
(\(\Omega\)) for mathematical Platonism or mistaking \emph{Centers of
Resolution} for panpsychism---this paper situates
\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\) rigorously within the philosophical and
physical tradition.

\subsubsection{2.1 Co-Defining First Principles: The ``Focus
vs.~Default''
Method}\label{co-defining-first-principles-the-focus-vs.-default-method}

A persistent error in foundational system-building is the Cartesian
demand to deduce an entire ontology from a single isolated axiom. As
Blaise Pascal recognized in \emph{De l'Esprit géométrique}, primitive
metaphysical principles---\emph{Being, Time, Space, Relation,
Potentiality, Evidence, Meaning, and Truth}---mutually co-define one
another. Interrogating all first principles simultaneously induces
analytical paralysis.

\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\) operates instead through \textbf{differential
philosophical focus}: when placing one first principle under the
analytical scalpel, adjacent principles are held provisionally steady
(``set by default'') in the background. For example, examining
\emph{Truth}---defined operationally as a determinate state of the world
at a locus together with all relational perspectives registered upon
it---immediately reveals silent dependencies on \emph{Chronometry},
\emph{Locality}, and \emph{Individuation}. Shifting the focal lens onto
\emph{Time} and \emph{Centers of Resolution} then resolves the
adjudication of colliding perspectives, feeding back to refine the
entire conceptual web.

\subsubsection{2.2 Ontic Reason vs.~Fallible
Rationality}\label{ontic-reason-vs.-fallible-rationality}

A second cornerstone of the framework's positioning is the strict
separation between \textbf{Ontic Reason} and \textbf{human rationality}:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Ontic Reason (\(X \to Y\)):} Reason is not a human cognitive
  faculty; it is \emph{what ontologically follows inside the substrate
  itself}. An apple tree yields apples, never oranges; an apple yields
  apple trees, never pear trees. Within nature, zero violations of Ontic

  transitions (\(A_s^v \to A_{s'}^{v+1}\)) with inviolable fidelity.
  Consequently, the phrase ``a flaw in nature'' is a contradiction in
  terms.
\item
  \textbf{Fallible Rationality:} Human rationality operates symbolically
  at a remove from the substrate and does not automatically track Ontic
  Reason. We can formulate an equation, introduce an ungrounded
  extrapolation (\(r \to 0\), \(\Delta\Omega = 0\),
  \(t \to \pm\infty\)), and arrive at an erroneous conclusion. Error and
  mathematical pathology belong exclusively to fallible human
  rationality drifting out of alignment with Ontic Reason, while
  subjective feelings of ``self-evidence'' carry zero epistemic weight

\end{itemize}

potentiality from either collapsing into determinism or inflating into

Cantor's completed infinite sets (\(\aleph_0\)) by defining
\textbf{Functional Infinity (\(\Omega\))} as the \emph{direct modal
expression of Ontic Reason}: the open-ended horizon of everything that
\emph{may ontologically follow} from an anchored entity \(A_s^v\) prior
to actualization, independent of whether that transition resolves
deterministically or probabilistically. Furthermore, where boundary
conditions lie beyond the operational reach of bound clocks---such as
the pre-Higgs regime prior to mass
nucleation---\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\) employs Immanuel Kant's
\textbf{abductive / regressive transcendental method}:
reverse-engineering the minimal maximal-entropy horizon (\(\Omega\))
required to sustain minimal-entropy condensation without reifying
unobservable sub-Higgs or superluminal entities as tangible physical
objects.

\subsection{3. Healing the Bergson--Einstein Schism: The Three-Tier
Architecture of
Time}\label{healing-the-bergsoneinstein-schism-the-three-tier-architecture-of-time}

On April 6, 1922, at the Société française de philosophie in Paris,
Henri Bergson and Albert Einstein clashed over whether physics captures
the reality of time. Bergson argued that relativity spatializes time
into a fourth coordinate dimension (\(x^0 = ct\)), missing lived
qualitative duration (\emph{durée}) and genuine simultaneity; Einstein
countered that there is no ``philosopher's time'' beyond physical clock
synchronization and psychological time.

Both thinkers were partially right and partially trapped by a false
binary: Bergson restricted \emph{durée} to biological consciousness,
while Einstein restricted physical time to external coordinate
signaling. From the foundational postulate of
\(\text{AEON-}T_{\mu\nu}\)---\emph{``If it exists, it is a
clock''}---follows a \textbf{Three-Tier Architecture of Time} that heals
this schism completely:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Tier I --- Pure Objective Cadence (\(\rho_\tau > 0\),
  Un-Clocked Existence):} Entities or radiative excitations may
  genuinely exist without constituting bound clocks in the strict
  subjective sense---such as a massless photon in free flight or the
  pre-metric conformal radiation pool (\(A_2^1\)). Lacking an internal
  rest-frame oscillator (\(\omega_C = 0\)), such modes possess zero
  proper-time perspective (\(d\tau = 0\)) and cannot act as receiving
  vertices; they are governed solely by the raw, un-anchored cadence of
  their necessary temporal intensity (\(\rho_\tau > 0\)).
\item
  \textbf{Tier II --- Object-Subjective Time (\emph{Object-as-Subject}
  Centers of Resolution):} Every mass-anchored, open-system clock
  (\(m > 0\), \(A_s^v\)) constitutes an irreducible \textbf{Center of
  Resolution} possessing its own intrinsic subjective time. Here,
  ``subjective time'' denotes not conscious human introspection, but the
  clock's own local proper-time perspective (\(dt_{\mathrm{pre}}\),
  \(v \to v + 1\)): the concrete relational vertex where arriving
  interactions across arbitrary cosmic distances (from a nearby lamp to
  an extinguished star ten billion light-years distant) funnel through
  Ontic Reason via \textbf{Two-Stage Condensation
  (\(\Phi_\tau(\Omega)\))} and instantiate \emph{``everything that is
  being to it''} within a finite \textbf{Operational Simultaneity
  Horizon (\(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}\))}. Relativistic proper time
  (\(d\tau\)) is literally the \emph{object-subjective time} of every
  bounded clock in the universe.
\item
  \textbf{Tier III --- Reflective Interiority (\emph{Subject-as-Subject}
  Lived Duration):} In conscious beings, the underlying
  \emph{object-subjective} clock-cadence of the physical organism is
  accompanied by qualitative interiority, which projects a
  transcendental \emph{now} outward across the horizon of Being and
  experiences perceived duration according to the density of novel
  meaning-condensations rather than the uniform tick-rate of external
  oscillators.
\end{enumerate}

This three-tier hierarchy eliminates panpsychism (atoms do not have
human thoughts, yet they possess irreducible \emph{object-subjective}
resolution vertices) while honoring lived interiority without severing
it from relativistic physics.
