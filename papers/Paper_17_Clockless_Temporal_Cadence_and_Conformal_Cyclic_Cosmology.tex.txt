\begin{abstract}
In Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC), Sir Roger Penrose demonstrated that the infinitely expanded, cold de Sitter future of one cosmic aeon is conformally equivalent to the hot, dense Big Bang of a subsequent aeon---provided that all rest-frame mass and extensive clocks vanish at the crossover hypersurface, leaving exclusively scale-invariant massless radiation. Yet standard physics struggles to explain the physical and thermodynamic mechanism by which a high-entropy, diluted void resets into a low-entropy origin without violating the Second Law of Thermodynamics. Drawing on a coherence-bounded ontology of time tested via the \texttt{AEON-T14.html} observatory, this paper provides the missing physical foundation for Penrose's cyclic crossover. By distinguishing \textbf{intensive temporal cadence} from \textbf{extensive clock duration}, we show that black hole Hawking evaporation and cosmic expansion progressively shed all localized rest frames, dissolving the \textbf{Operational Simultaneity Horizon} ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}(r)$) and eliminating every macroscopic plane of interaction. Once all observer planes dissolve, spent classical scaffolding returns to an \textbf{instant, immediate pool of possibilities}---allowing a fresh phase-locking cascade ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} > 0$) to unfold a new aeon without carrying forward the thermal entropy of its predecessor.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: Penrose's Conformal Insight and Its Physical Gap}

\subsection{The Genius of Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC)}
Sir Roger Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology \cite{penrose2010} rests on a remarkable geometric truth of general relativity: null light cones---which define causal structure---are completely invariant under conformal rescalings of the spacetime metric ($g_{\mu\nu} \to \Omega^2 g_{\mu\nu}$). Only rest mass ($m_0 > 0$) introduces a physical scale via Compton frequency ($f_{\mathrm{local}} = m_0 c^2 / h$), anchoring localized clocks that measure extensive proper duration ($d\tau > 0$). Penrose recognized that if all massive particles decay or evaporate into massless radiation in the remote future of an accelerating universe, the metric loses all sense of spatial and temporal scale---allowing the remote future infinity ($\mathscr{I}^+$) of one aeon to join smoothly onto the Big Bang boundary ($\mathscr{B}$) of the next.

\subsection{The Two Open Questions Facing CCC}
Despite its mathematical elegance, CCC faces two persistent objections: (1) \emph{Why does ``infinitely large and cold'' physically behave identically to ``infinitely small and hot''?} and (2) \emph{How does thermodynamic entropy reset at the crossover without violating conservation laws?} Using the operational spacetime framework of \texttt{AEON-T14.html} \cite{damasio_compendium,damasio_aeont14,damasio_p15,damasio_p16}, we show that both questions resolve naturally once time is understood as intensive cadence rather than extensive clock duration.

\section{Shedding Clocks at Both Cosmic Boundaries}

\subsection{Why Mass Anchors Duration While Massless Cadence Remains Scale-Free}
In \texttt{AEON-T14.html}, an extensive rest-frame clock exists only when a field possesses non-zero rest mass ($m_0 > 0$) or localized confinement, generating a proper Compton oscillation rate $f_{\mathrm{local}} = m_0 c^2 / h$ and projecting a finite \textbf{Operational Simultaneity Horizon} ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}}(r) > 0$). By contrast, a massless photon ($m_0 = 0, \gamma_{\mathrm{pre}} \to \infty$) travels from emission to absorption with zero proper duration ($d\tau = 0$) and zero spatial simultaneity plane ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} \to 0$), yet retains pure, scale-invariant \textbf{temporal cadence} ($\Phi_\tau = 1$).

\subsection{The Far-Future de Sitter Limit ($L \to L_H$): Shedding All Localized Clocks}
As a cosmic aeon evolves into its late accelerating de Sitter phase \cite{gh1977}, two processes systematically strip away every physical clock in the universe:

\begin{enumerate}

Spent classical scaffolding dissolves back into pre-metric potential.
    \item \textbf{Drawing Fresh Cadence From the Immediate Pool:} Reality does not recycle worn-out thermal debris from a finite past; rather, every actualized state crystallizes directly out of an \textbf{instant, immediate pool of possibilities (a functional infinity)} governed by invariant conservation rails ($\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and $\rho_\tau \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} = \mathrm{const.}$) \cite{damasio_p16}. When a new phase-locking cascade opens $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sim}} > 0$ at the onset of the next aeon, it draws fresh, low-entropy temporal cadence immediately from that unbounded potential pool.
\end{enumerate}
