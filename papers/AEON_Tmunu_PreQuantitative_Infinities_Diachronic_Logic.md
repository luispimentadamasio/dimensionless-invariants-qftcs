### 1.2 Essential Homogeneity vs. Heterogeneous Mathematics

A foundational error in modern physics is conflating the **original infinities of Time and Space** with **mathematical infinities** (such as Georg Cantor’s transfinite cardinals $\aleph_0, \aleph_1, \dots$ or the real-number continuum $\mathbb{R}$):

* **Mathematical Infinities Are Internally Heterogeneous:** Set-theoretic and geometric infinities are constructed out of distinguishable elements, subsets, or cuts. Inside a set, any two elements are either distinct ($x \neq y$) or collapse into the exact same element ($x = y$). Mathematics cannot count, integrate, or axiomatize without first carving discrete internal boundaries.
* **Original Time and Space Are Essentially Homogeneous Within:** Pre-metric Time and Space are not aggregates of zero-width instants ($t_0$) or dimensionless points ($x_0$). Internally, they are seamless, undivided, and non-quantitative—**essentially homogeneous within**. Dropping a coordinate grid $(x^\mu)$ onto them replaces an undivided ground with a heterogeneous scaffolding of epistemic projections. Consequently, quantitative mathematics can never directly grasp the original infinities; it operates exclusively upon finite partitions carved *within* them.

### 2.1 Extensional Residue vs. Intensional Path-Adequacy

In classical arithmetic, equality (`=`) is purely extensional:
$$5 - 5 = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad 6 - 6 = 0 \implies 5 - 5 = 6 - 6.$$
Inside $\mathcal{D}_\infty$, however, **how a determination is reached belongs to the determination itself**:

* Both `5 - 5` and `6 - 6` yield the same terminal residue (`0`), just as `2 - 1` and `3 - 2` both yield `1`.
* Yet to describe what happened to Adam’s basket, `5 - 5 = 0` is **ontologically adequate** (recording the real diachronic transition $A_5^1 \to A_0^2$), whereas `6 - 6 = 0` is **ontologically inadequate** because it falsifies the initial state.
* Similarly, Kurt Gödel’s incompleteness theorems reflect this exact boundary: any finite axiomatic sieve carved out of $\mathcal{D}_\infty$ necessarily leaves valid determinations outside its deductive reach.

### 2.2 Modal Logic of Diachronic Identity ($A_s^v$) and Minimal-Entropy Actualization

Classical predicate logic relies on Leibniz’s Law and static reflexivity ($A = A$), which paralyzes identity across time: either $A$ changes and ceases to be $A$, or $A$ remains frozen outside duration. **Diachronic Identity ($A_s^v$)** resolves this by indexing every actualized entity with two distinct coordinates:

* **Sequential Verification Ordinal ($v$):** The irreversible step-by-step index of temporal actualization ($v = 1, 2, 3, \dots$).

1. **Permanence as Active Change ($A_1^1 \to A_1^2 \to A_1^3$):** Enduring as the "same" qualitative state ($s = 1$) across time requires continuous ordinal re-actualization ($v \to v+1$). *Permanence is ontologically identical to change.*

Consider a physical oscillator or engine alternating between two qualitative states in a closed cycle:
$$A_1^1 \longrightarrow A_2^1 \longrightarrow A_1^2 \longrightarrow A_2^2 \longrightarrow A_1^3 \longrightarrow A_2^3 \longrightarrow \dots$$
