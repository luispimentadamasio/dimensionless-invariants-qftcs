\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

Standard Big Bang cosmology encounters an acute conceptual and
mathematical impasse at the boundary \(t = 0\): extrapolating smooth
pseudo-Riemannian manifold geometry (\(\mathbb{R}^4\)) and atomic clock
units backward into the Planck regime produces unphysical curvature
singularities (\(r = 0\), \(\rho \to \infty\)) while presupposing a
quantitative time ruler in an epoch devoid of bound physical clocks.
Building upon the \emph{Theory of Objective Time}
(\(\text{AEON-T}_{\mu\nu}\)) and its pre-axiomatic diachronic logic,
this paper constructs a singularity-free cosmological reconstruction
across the cosmic aeon crossover formalized as
\(A = A_1^1 \to A_2^1 \to A_3^1\). By separating \emph{pre-metric
temporal cadence} (\(\rho_\tau > 0\))---the intrinsic, self-expressing
rhythm of objective time within its three-way transcendental
domain---from \emph{earned metric duration} (\(dt_{\mathrm{pre}}\)), we
resolve the apparent paradox of pre-Big-Bang temporality: in the
clockless conformal crossover state (\(A_2^1\)), quantitative intervals
collapse into scale-indifference
(\(10^{-24}\,\mathrm{s} \equiv 10^{12}\,\mathrm{yr}\)) while active
temporal cadence persists continuously. Combining Roger Penrose's
conformal spatial rescaling with two-stage operational condensation
(\(\Phi_\tau(\Omega)\)), we trace how the massless radiation pool of a
prior aeon crosses \(A_2^1\) and nucleates mass-bearing bound clocks
(\(A_3^1\)), mapping six evolutionary stages that sharply distinguish
retroactive metric projections (\(t_{\mathrm{retro}}\)) from genuinely
earned clock duration (\(t_{\mathrm{earned}}\)) ignited at electroweak
symmetry breaking (\(A = A_1^1 \to A_1^2\)).

\textbf{Keywords:} Philosophy of Time, Objective Time
(\(\text{AEON-T}_{\mu\nu}\)), Diachronic Logic, Pre-Metric Duration,
Conformal Cyclic Cosmology, Quantum-Gravity Wall, Operational
Regularization.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{1. Introduction: The Chronometric Impasse at
\(t = 0\)}{1. Introduction: The Chronometric Impasse at t = 0}}\label{introduction-the-chronometric-impasse-at-t-0}

Modern physical cosmology achieves extraordinary empirical precision
from Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (\(t \sim 1\,\mathrm{s}\)) through Cosmic
Microwave Background (CMB) recombination
(\(t \sim 380{,}000\,\mathrm{yr}\)) to late-time structure formation.
Yet when classical General Relativity is extrapolated backward toward
\(t \to 0\), the formalism suffers a dual breakdown:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Geometric Divergence:} Curvature invariants diverge
  (\(R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} \to \infty\)), signaling
  the breakdown of smooth manifold calculus (\(\mathbb{R}^4\)).
\item
  \textbf{Chronometric Circularity:} Standard accounts routinely quote
  timestamps such as \(10^{-43}\,\mathrm{s}\) (Planck epoch) or
  \(10^{-36}\,\mathrm{s}\) (GUT scale) as though an operational
  stopwatch were ticking at \(t = 0\). However, physical seconds are
  defined by periodic transitions in bound, mass-bearing quantum systems
  (such as hyperfine atomic oscillators). Prior to the nucleation of
  rest mass and bound structures, no physical reference clocks exist to
  slice duration into seconds.
\end{enumerate}

Traditional metaphysics compounds this difficulty through a false
dichotomy: either time itself ``began'' out of a timeless nothingness
(\(\varnothing\))---leaving any causal transition into existence
unintelligible---or an infinite, pre-existing clock-grid
(\(t \in (-\infty, +\infty)\)) is projected backward into an eternal
past, generating severe thermodynamic fine-tuning and Boltzmann-brain
paradoxes.

Within the \emph{Theory of Objective Time} (\(\text{AEON-T}_{\mu\nu}\))
{[}1, 2{]}, this dilemma dissolves once we recognize that nothing exists
outside of time and that our mathematical coordinate grids are secondary
cartographic projections built atop an active temporal substrate. To
exist at all is to possess \textbf{temporal cadence}---the intrinsic,
self-expressing rhythm of objective time (\(ho_   au > 0\)). Metric
duration (\(dt_{\mathrm{pre}}\)), by contrast, is an operational
quantity \emph{earned} only when bounded, open-system physical clocks
(\emph{``If it is, it is a clock''}) condense and exchange causal
signals across finite separations.

dissolve. \(A_1^1\) represents the final operational boundary where
metric distinctions fade out.
